# Hoop Dynasty

Hoop Dynasty is a free online basketball card game. Draft a starting five of real NBA stars, open packs to collect 540+ cards from Common to Immortal, and train your players into legends. Win seasons, climb Ranked against real players' teams, challenge friends to 1v1s, trade cards, and customize your profile. Build your dynasty!

**Play:** https://sephurs.github.io/Hoop-Dynasty/
